A premium, feature-rich snake game with modern graphics, power-ups, multiple game modes, and a deep progression system.

Goal: Eat food to grow and score points. Avoid walls, obstacles, and your own tail.
Controls: Swipe, keyboard arrows (WASD), or D-Pad.
