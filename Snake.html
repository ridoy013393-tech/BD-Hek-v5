ℹ️ About
🐍 Snake Evolution
A premium, feature-rich snake game with modern graphics, power-ups, multiple game modes, and a deep progression system. Evolve your snake, collect coins, unlock skins, and conquer challenges!
🎯 How to Play
Goal: Eat food to grow and score points. Avoid walls, obstacles, and your own tail.
Controls: Swipe, keyboard arrows (WASD), or D-Pad.
Tips: Build combos for bonus points. Collect power-ups for advantages. Unlock new skins and modes as you level up!
📌 Controls
🖱️ Keyboard: Arrow Keys / WASD
👆 Touch: Swipe on screen
🎮 D-Pad: On-screen buttons
⏸️ Pause: Tap pause button or press Space/Escape
💡 Tips & Tricks
• Build long combos for massive score multipliers
• Use power-ups strategically in tough situations
• Complete missions for extra coins and rewards
• Try different game modes for varied challenges
• Collect daily rewards to boost your progress
📱 New Player Guide
1. Start with Classic mode to learn the basics.
2. Eat food to grow your snake and earn points.
3. Avoid hitting walls or your own tail.
4. Collect coins to unlock skins and power-ups.
5. Level up to unlock new game modes and features!
